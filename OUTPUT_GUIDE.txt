🎯 FASTEST WAY TO SEE RESULTS (WEB BROWSER - RECOMMENDED):
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: Activate environment
  source venv/Scripts/activate

Step 2: Launch web interface
  streamlit run app_improved.py

Step 3: Browser opens automatically at http://localhost:8501
  
Step 4: Upload foggy image
  - Click "📤 Upload Image"
  - Drag & drop your image or click to browse
  
Step 5: Click "🔄 Process Image"
  - Takes 30-60 seconds on CPU
  - Shows real-time progress spinner
  
Step 6: See results
  - LEFT: Original foggy image
  - RIGHT: Restored clear image
  - METRICS: Brightness & contrast values
  
Step 7: Download
  - Click "⬇️ Download Restored Image"
  - Or download "Before/After Comparison"
